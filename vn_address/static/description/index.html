Vietnam Administrative Units

34 provinces and 3,321 communes, wards and special zones after the 2025 reform, ready to use and kept up to date

Key features

  • Every commune, ward and special zone of Vietnam (Decision 19/2025/QD-TTg) as an Odoo city, with its 5-digit administrative code, level and province.
  • The 34 provinces of Odoo get their official administrative code and level.
  • Data available right after installation, without internet access.
  • One-click synchronisation from the AddressKit API (source: National Statistics Office of Vietnam), or a weekly scheduled action.
  • Safe updates: units that disappear are archived, never deleted, so existing addresses keep their value; a partial or malformed answer from the API is rejected without changing anything.
  • English and Vietnamese interface.

Synchronise with one click

Administrators can refresh the list from Contacts/Configuration/Vietnam: Communes / Wards. The result shows how many units were created, updated, archived and restored.

Pick the commune on your contacts

Enable Enforce Cities on Vietnam (Contacts/Configuration/Countries) to select the commune or ward from the official list on every address.

Good to know

  • Province names are replaced by the official short names from the source.
  • Uninstalling deletes the communes loaded by the module; contacts keep their address text.
  • Data source: AddressKit, based on the National Statistics Office of Vietnam.
